# Apparat — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Apparat (package `com.omniforge.app`) is a toolkit for working with PDFs, photos, video, audio and
text on your Android device.

## What the app collects
Nothing. Apparat has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does
not request the internet permission, so it cannot send your files or any data about you anywhere.

## Your files
Files you pick or share into Apparat are processed on your device. Results are kept in the app's
private cache until you save or share them; saved files go to `Downloads/Apparat` on your device.
Temporary copies are deleted automatically. When you choose "Modify Original", a temporary backup
of the original is kept in the app's cache only while it is being replaced, then deleted.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: to show progress and "finished" notices for long jobs.
- **Foreground service (data sync / media processing)**: so long jobs keep running if you switch apps.

Apparat does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions. It reads
only the files you choose through Android's file picker or share menu.

## Backups
Android may back up the app's settings (theme, default save mode, shortcuts) with your device
backup. Your files are not part of that backup.

## Children
Apparat is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.
